How to use

Following the brand's directions, with a patch test first and sunscreen in the morning.

  1. Patch test first. Apply a little behind the ear or on the jawline and wait 24 hours.
  2. Cleanse. Wash your face well and pat it dry.
  3. Massage in. Take a small amount and massage it into the face until it absorbs. Avoid the eyes and lips.
  4. Wait, then layer. After about 10 minutes, apply a second thin layer like a night mask and leave it on overnight.
  5. Rinse in the morning. Wash off with cold water, then apply SPF 30+ sunscreen. Use nightly.

Not recommended for children under 14, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for elderly users, unless a doctor approves. Do not apply on broken, sunburnt or irritated skin. Stop if you get burning, swelling or a rash, and see a doctor.
