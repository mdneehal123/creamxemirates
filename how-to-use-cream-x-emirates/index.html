How to Use Cream X Emirates: Night Routine, Step by Step

Updated 2026-11-06 · General cosmetic guidance, not medical advice.

Use it at night: cleanse and dry your face, massage in a small amount until absorbed, wait about 10 minutes, apply a second thin layer as an overnight mask, and rinse with cold water in the morning. Wear sunscreen every day.

Step by step

  1. Patch test. Apply a little behind the ear or on the jawline and wait 24 hours before the first full use.
  2. Cleanse. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry. Cream absorbs better on clean, dry skin.
  3. First layer. Take a small amount, dot it on the forehead, cheeks, nose and chin, and massage until absorbed. Avoid eyes and lips.
  4. Wait 10 minutes. Let the first layer settle.
  5. Overnight layer. Spread a second thin layer like a mask and sleep with it.
  6. Morning. Rinse with cold water, moisturise if needed, and apply SPF 30+ sunscreen.

How much to use

A small amount, about the size of a pea for each layer, is enough for the face. Using more does not work faster and can feel heavy.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping sunscreen: new tan keeps forming and hides progress.
  • Applying on broken, sunburnt or freshly waxed skin.
  • Mixing with other strong creams, peels or bleach on the same night.
  • Stopping and starting: results need regular use for 8 to 12 weeks.

Who should not use it

The brand does not recommend it for children under 14, pregnant women or elderly people. Ask a doctor first if you are breastfeeding or have a skin condition.

Questions

Can I use Cream X Emirates in the daytime?

The brand's routine is for night use. In the day, wear sunscreen.

Should I wash it off in the morning?

Yes. Rinse with cold water in the morning, then apply sunscreen.

Can I use it every day?

The brand recommends daily use. If your skin feels dry or tingly, use it every other night at first.
