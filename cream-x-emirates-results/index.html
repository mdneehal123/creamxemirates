Cream X Emirates Results: How Many Days, and What to Expect

Updated 2026-11-06 · General cosmetic guidance, not medical advice.

Most people notice softer skin within 2 to 4 weeks, and a brighter, more even look after 8 to 12 weeks of nightly use with daily sunscreen. Faster "7-day" promises are not realistic for any cream.

Week by week

Week 1 to 2Skin adjusts; feels softer. Mild tingling possible.
Week 3 to 4Smoother texture, fresher look in the morning.
Week 5 to 8Dullness improves; light tan starts to look lighter.
Week 8 to 12A more even look on tan and uneven patches.

What speeds things up

  • Sunscreen SPF 30+ every morning, reapplied outdoors
  • Using it every night, not on and off
  • A gentle face wash, not harsh scrubs

What slows things down

  • Daily sun without protection
  • Lemon, bleach or harsh home remedies
  • Picking pimples, which leaves marks

Results vary with skin type, the depth of tan and daily sun exposure. A face cream does not change your natural skin colour.

Questions

Can Cream X Emirates give results in 7 days?

Skin can feel softer quickly, but visible change in tan or uneven tone takes 8 to 12 weeks with sunscreen.

What happens if I stop using it?

Nothing harmful. Without sunscreen, tan can come back over time.

Do I need to finish the whole jar?

One jar lasts about 4 to 6 weeks; most people need 2 to 3 jars to judge results properly.
