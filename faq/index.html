How do I use Cream X Emirates?

At night, cleanse and dry your face, massage in a small amount until absorbed, wait about 10 minutes, then apply a second thin layer as an overnight mask. Rinse with cold water in the morning and use sunscreen.

How soon will I see results?

Skin often feels softer within a few weeks. Any change in tan or uneven tone is gradual, usually over 8 to 12 weeks, and depends on daily sunscreen. Results vary from person to person.

Who should not use it?

The brand does not recommend it for children under 14, pregnant women or elderly people. Ask a doctor first if you are breastfeeding or have a skin condition, and never apply it on broken or irritated skin.
